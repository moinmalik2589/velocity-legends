# Roadmap

1. Foundation: versioning, feature flags, tests, documentation, shared schemas.
2. UI and offline profile hardening.
3. Data-driven garage, economy, career, missions, and achievements.
4. Three.js 3D race renderer and procedural track/vehicle assets.
5. Backend-ready API, cloud-save, and ghost-race contracts.
6. Capacitor Android wrapper and release assets/checklists.

External credentials, signing keys, Play Console identity, support contact details, and payment accounts remain owner-supplied requirements.
